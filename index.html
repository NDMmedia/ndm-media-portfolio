Have a question that is not covered here? Send a message and tell us about your project.

What kind of videos do you edit?

NDM Media focuses on podcast clips, talking-head videos and long-form content. Share your project details so we can confirm whether it fits our services.

Do you add background music?

Editing is tailored to the brief. For podcast and talking-head content, clarity of voice and natural pacing come first; music is only included when it suits the project and is requested.

How do I get a quote?

Email us with your video length, number of deliverables, preferred style, deadline and any reference links. We can then discuss the scope and next steps.

Are the portfolio samples ready?

Yes. The two sample edits, a podcast clip and a talking-head video, are playing in the Selected work section.

Do you offer a free sample edit?

No free sample edit is advertised on this website. To discuss a project, please contact us with your requirements and we will clarify the scope and terms before work begins.
